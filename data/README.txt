Take-home case-001 — data and attribution

Files in this folder:

- case-001-dicom.zip — CT series, 180 axial DICOM slices.
- case-001-reference-lesions.nii.gz — reference (manual / ground-truth) binary lesion segmentation (values 0 and 1), in the same geometry as the masks returned by the take-home API.

The CT and the reference lesion segmentation are a subset of a public dataset:

Wasserthal, J. (2026). Liver lesions CT training dataset. Zenodo.
